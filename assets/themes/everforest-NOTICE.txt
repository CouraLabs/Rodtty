Everforest palette used by Rodtty's Appearances.

Primary source: https://github.com/sainnhe/everforest (retrieved upstream
values, see sources.json for the pinned records)

Everforest license declaration (upstream): MIT + third-party licenses
("MIT + Third-party" per its LICENSE file, covering Vim color scheme ports).
Rodtty uses the light and dark palettes' literal RGB values for UI surfaces,
terminal ANSI slots and editor syntax roles, with small-text contrast adapted
toward the family ink as documented in README.md.

Upstream permission statement:
https://github.com/sainnhe/everforest/blob/master/LICENSE.md

The palette name "Everforest" belongs to its creator; Rodtty only adapts the
literal color values, with attribution kept in Settings next to each
appearance.

Terminal ANSI slots follow the terminal color mapping in colors/everforest.vim
over the medium palette in autoload/everforest.vim, pinned in sources.json.
