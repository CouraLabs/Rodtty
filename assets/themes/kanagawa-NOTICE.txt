Kanagawa palettes used by Rodtty's Appearances.

Primary source: https://github.com/rebelot/kanagawa.nvim (retrieved upstream
values, see sources.json for the pinned records)

Kanagawa license declaration (upstream): distributed under the MIT License.

terminal ANSI slots and editor syntax roles, with small-text contrast adapted
toward the family ink as documented in README.md.
